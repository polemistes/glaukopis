# 0067 — The contributors of a document, where a line begins, the numbering of lines in two windows, and the bracket

Date: 2026-10-24. Status: accepted by the owner on 2026-10-24, from their comments after the build of 0066, an assessment with recommendations, and their four resolutions of it (contributors with roles rather than a translators field; the writer decides which part of a shared line is counted; spaces for Word and Writer; the side of the annotations a choice); to be built from `docs/plans/contributors-where-a-line-begins-and-the-numbering-of-lines.md`. Amends 0055 (the title page: the roles), 0057 (the items of the header and the footer: editor and translator), 0039 and 0041 (the numbers of lines: which are shown, two windows, one kind counted, where a line begins, the side of the annotations), 0040 (the indent of a paragraph: a length, and on the page for every kind), 0054 (Document properties: the contributors, no hints), 0065 (the words).

## Context

The owner, translating Greek drama, after the build of 0066: a translation of an old text has the author in its title (*Aristophanes: Lysistrata*) and no author of its own, and a translator where the author would stand, *translated by …*, *oversatt av …*; a translation of a modern work has both. A general thing, not one made for them. No more help texts in menus and dialogs; the labels speak for themselves. A line of verse shared by two speakers: the second speech begins in the middle of the line, and is set in so that it begins where the first ended; the owner asks how, wanting a general way of saying how far a line is set in, not a function for this one case. The window of the line numbers confuses: its help texts go, and it is to be two windows under two menu items, *Line numbering from here* and *Numbering of this line*. Where every fifth number is shown and the count begins at 1, the 1 is not shown, nor 41 where it begins at 41: 45 is the first number shown. The first window gets a choice to count the lines of one kind of paragraph only. A number given by hand with letters in it, *11b*, is to be followed by 12. *Words in the margin* becomes *Annotation in the right margin*. And the thin line in the margin that names the kind of a run sometimes jumps further into the page for a part of its length, over the numbers of the lines; the owner has not seen when.

On the assessment, the owner: the authors of a document are to be a list of contributors with roles, author, editor, translator, now rather than later; the setting-in of a line must not make it uncounted, since the line's own window has *Counted* and the writer decides which part of a shared line is counted, traditions differing (some count the first part, some the last); an approximately right number of spaces is the right thing for Word and Writer, being what their publisher's typesetters want; and the annotations were seen at the right also where the numbers stood at the left: their side should perhaps be a choice.

What was found:

- **The persons of a document.** The details of a document (`DocumentSettings` in `project/model/types.ts`) are the title, the subtitle, the authors, the keywords, the date and the identifiers; an author is a person with a name, an affiliation, an address and an ORCID (`Person`), and the core's `Document` has `authors: Vec<Author>` and no other persons. The title page is made in one place for Word, Writer, LaTeX and the web (`title_page` in `export/mod.rs`) and by show rules for Typst (`<gk-authors>`, `<gk-affiliations>` in `formats/typst.rs`); the header and the footer know the items page number, title, author, chapter, date and own words (`Item` in `formats/mod.rs`); Word and Writer have the paragraph styles Title, Subtitle, Author, Affiliation and Date (`export/reference.rs`); the files' properties name the authors as creators (`after.rs`, `dc:creator`). The words a document prints in its own language, *Notes*, *Abstract*, *Contents*, come from `locales/<language>/document.ftl`, which 25 languages have, English standing in where a language lacks a word (`document::term`): *edited by* and *translated by* can be said in the language of the document the same way. The Org form writes the authors as an `AUTHORS` property (`org/properties.ts`); the search reads their names (`search/everything.ts`); a project that begins is given its maker as author (`migrate.ts`). The library knows the roles editor and translator in references (BibTeX, CSL, MARC, Zotero), which has nothing to do with the document's own details.
- **Shared lines.** A line of verse is set in by steps (`indent` in its line mark, none to eight, Tab and Shift+Tab), each 1.5 em on the page (`gk-line` in `typst.rs`, `\hspace*` in LaTeX, four non-breaking spaces in Word and Writer) and in the text view (`app.css`). A paragraph of any other kind is set in by steps as a whole (`paragraph.indent`, Tab), which the page honours only for lists (`list_from` in `document/passages.rs`; `kind_paragraph` reads no indent): a Tab in prose shows where one writes and not on the page. No length can make the second half of a shared line begin where the first ended, since that depends on the words of the first and the font; Typst measures content (`measure`, in a `context`), LaTeX with `\settowidth`, and a page of the web can set the same words invisible before the line; Word and Writer measure nothing. The owner's Antigone is text paragraphs with line breaks, one paragraph a speech, the speaker a kind of words at its head; so the line before the second half is the last line of the paragraph before, and in a document of the Play type a speaker's paragraph may stand between them. The same setting-in is what Mayakovsky's ladder and Williams's stepped lines want, and what dialogue in verse wants in every language: a general thing.
- **The numbering.** One window (`LineNumbersDialog.svelte`) asks for the stretch (numbering, number from, every how many, side) and for the line the cursor is in (number, not counted, hidden, words in the margin), with seven hints. A number given by hand is always shown (`Shown { set: true }` in `document/numbers.rs` and `project/model/lines.ts`), and the count goes on from its last run of digits: after *567a* comes 568, after *1094a1* comes *1094a2*, in both counters, with tests that say so (`go_on_from_the_digits_of_a_label_keeping_what_stands_before_them`); by these rules *11b* is followed by 12. The owner saw otherwise; the case must be reproduced before anything is changed (a line that is not counted, a stretch numbered *only the numbers given*, or something the field in the margin does). What a numbering is set on is the selection, else the run the cursor is in, the whole of it, the paragraphs before the cursor too (`stretchToNumber` in `editor/numbers.ts`). The count takes a line in when the kind of its paragraph is counted, which is a fact of the kind (text, lines of verse and items are; speakers, directions and attributions are not: `formats/kinds.rs`, `families.ts`); nothing says *only this kind*.
- **The annotations.** The words in the margin stand across from the numbers, by design and in both views: `beside` in `passages.rs` puts them at the right where the numbers stand at the left and at the left where the numbers stand at the right, `gk-line` the same (`other`), and `app.css` moves `.line-margin` to the left under `p[data-numbers='right']`. What the owner saw, annotations at the right with numbers at the left, is that rule; no choice exists, and whether the right-hand case holds in the owner's documents is to be checked in the harness.
- **The bracket.** The line of a bracket is a widget in each paragraph of the run, placed from that paragraph's own box (`.run-line` in `app.css`, `left: calc(-10px - var(--inset))`), and moved out past the numbers only on the paragraphs that lie in a numbered stretch (`p[data-numbers='left'] > .run-line`, 3.4 em). A run and a stretch are not the same thing: a run is paragraphs of one kind standing together, a stretch the paragraphs numbered alike. Where a run holds paragraphs that are not numbered, as after numbering a selection, or paragraphs put in afterwards, the segments beside the unnumbered paragraphs stand some 3 em further in, through the column of the numbers. For text, which has a bracket only where it is numbered, the run is every paragraph of text standing together, which may be the whole text: numbering a few paragraphs in the middle draws a bracket along all of it, jumping where the numbering stops. This is read from the rules; the plan has the builder confirm it in the harness first.

## Decision

### The contributors

- **The authors become contributors with roles.** A document's persons are one list, each with a role, *Author*, *Editor* or *Translator*, and a name, an affiliation, an address and an ORCID as before. Stored as `contributors` with `role` on each (`author`, `editor`, `translator`); a project's `authors` are read once as contributors of the role author, and the maker of a new project is its first contributor as today. Document properties, Details, shows *Contributors*: a row each with the role first, then the fields of today, *Add a contributor*, remove; inherited from the element over as the authors were.
- **On the title page** the authors stand where they stand today, with the lines of their affiliations; then one line for the editors, *Edited by A and B*, and one for the translators, *Translated by C*, in that order, before the date; where there are no authors, these lines stand in the authors' place; where the same persons edited and translated, one line, *Edited and translated by A*. The words are the document's language's (`document-edited-by`, `document-translated-by`, `document-edited-and-translated-by`, `document-and` in `document.ftl`), with a capital, each being a line of its own. In every kind of document that is made: Typst by a show rule set as the authors are; Word and Writer in a paragraph style *Role*, made as *Author* is; LaTeX within `\author` after the authors' lines; a page of the web and an e-book as `.gk-roles`; the head of a Markdown file as `editor` and `translator`. The files' properties name the authors as creators and the editors and translators as contributors where the format has such a field (Word and Writer `dc:contributor`; the e-book's package with their roles). The affiliations printed are the authors', as today; what an editor or translator has beyond a name is kept and not printed.
- **Look, Front page, Include** gains *Editors and translators*, on unless the layout says otherwise, beside *Authors* and *Their affiliations*; *Anonymize the front page* leaves every contributor out, in the running head and the properties too.
- **The header and the footer** gain the items *Editor* and *Translator*: the names, as *Author* gives the authors'.
- **The Org form** writes `AUTHORS`, `EDITORS` and `TRANSLATORS`, each as `AUTHORS` is written today, and reads them back into one list in that order; a project whose Org form has `AUTHORS` alone reads as before. **The search** finds a contributor's name as it found an author's.
- **The three hints of Details go** (*What stands on the first page…*, *Left empty, the name of the element is the title*, the hint under *Number the lines of the page*), the window being a dialog like the others; the placeholder of the title already says *The name of the element*, and the switch of the page's lines is named *Number every line of the printed page*.

### Where a line begins

- **A line may begin where the line before ended.** It is a setting of the line, kept in its line mark (`continues`), turned on and off with ***Begins where the line before ended*** in the right-click menu of the text, which shows a check while it holds. The line before is the nearest line above it that has words in it, in the paragraph before where the line is the first of its paragraph, speakers and stage directions passed over. On the page the line is set in by the width of the words of that line and an em, in the PDF, the PDF set by LaTeX and the page of the web exactly, by measuring; in the text view the same, measured from what is drawn; Word and Writer, which measure nothing, set in about two non-breaking spaces for each character of the line before, as they set verse in with spaces today, which is what the owner's publisher's typesetters want. Steps given with Tab add to it.
- **The count is the writer's.** The setting-in changes nothing of the count: which part of a shared line is counted the writer says in *Numbering of this line*, traditions differing (some count the first part, some the last).
- **The step** of Tab and Shift+Tab is a length of the layout, ***Indentation step*** under *Paragraphs* in Look, 0.6 cm in the layouts that ship (close to the 1.5 em of today), used by the text view and by every kind of document that is made, in verse and in any paragraph set in with Tab.
- **Steps reach the page for every kind.** A paragraph set in with Tab is set in by so many steps in the PDF, by LaTeX, in the web page and the e-book, and with non-breaking spaces in Word and Writer, as lists and lines of verse are today; today the text view shows it and the page does not.

### The numbering of lines, in two windows

- ***Line numbering from here…*** in the right-click menu of the text, and ***Line numbering…*** in the menu of a bracket, open the first window, whose settings hold from the paragraph the cursor is in: on the paragraphs selected, where several are; else from the cursor's paragraph to the end of the stretch it is in, or, where it is not numbered yet, to the end of its run (its kind standing together, speakers and directions among them). The paragraphs before keep their numbering. From a bracket's menu the cursor goes to the run's first paragraph first, so that the whole run is meant. Its rows: *Numbering* (*Not numbered*, *Counted from the first number given*, *Only the numbers given*); *Number from*, filled in with the number the first counted line from here has now, given or counted, 1 where it has none; *Show a number every*; *Numbers on the*; and ***Count the lines of***: *Every kind*, or one kind of paragraph, chosen among the kinds of the paragraphs the settings hold for, speakers and directions left out since they are never counted. No hints.
- ***Numbering of this line…*** in the right-click menu opens the second: the words of the line, as today; *Its number or label*; ***Counted***, on unless the line says otherwise; ***Its number is shown***: *As the others are*, *Always*, *Never*; and ***Annotation in the right margin***, or *in the left margin*, the label naming the side the annotation goes to under the layout. No hints. In a paragraph of a kind that is never counted, as a speaker, only the annotation is asked.
- **Which numbers are shown.** A counted number shows where the count divides by *every*. A number given by hand follows the same rule: a count that begins at 1 or at 41 shows nothing on that line, and 5 or 45 is the first number shown; a number given to mend the count in the middle shows only where it falls on *every*. A label that holds anything but digits, *567a*, *11b*, *§5*, *\**, is always shown, since no count can make it. The writer says otherwise for a line with *Always* and *Never*, the latter being today's *Counted, its number not shown*. Where one writes, a number given by hand stands dark as today, so that one sees which numbers one gave, and in parentheses where the page shows none, as held numbers do.
- **The count after a label** goes on from its last run of digits, *11b* to 12, *1094a1* to *1094a2*, as both counters already do; the owner's case is reproduced through the field in the margin and through the window, in the text view and on the page, and mended where it fails.
- ***Count the lines of* one kind** is a setting of the stretch, kept on its paragraphs with the others (`only`), part of what makes paragraphs number together; lines of the other kinds are passed over as speakers are, and show only what was given them by hand.
- **The side of the annotations is the layout's choice.** Look, under the numbers of lines, gains ***Annotations on the***: *Other side from the numbers*, as today and in the layouts that ship, *Left*, or *Right*. Where the annotations stand on the numbers' side, they stand outside the numbers, further from the text, on the page and where one writes. The line's window names the side that results. The harness checks both views with the numbers at the right.
- **The words**: *Line numbering from here*, *Numbering of this line*, *Counted*, *Its number is shown*, *Annotation in the right margin*, *Count the lines of*, *Every kind*, *Annotations on the*; the name of the stored field stays `margin`.

### The bracket

- **The bracket of a run stands outside the numbers wherever any paragraph of the run is numbered at the left**, along the whole run, so that it never jumps within one run; and **for text the bracket follows the numbered stretch**, not the run of text, so that numbering a few paragraphs brackets those and no others. The segments of one bracket are measured against each other in the harness.

## Choices made in writing this, which the owner has not been asked about

1. **Three roles**, author, editor and translator, as the owner named them; the role is a stored word, so that others (illustrator, commentator) can come without changing the shape.
2. **Each role a line of its own, in a fixed order**: the authors as today, the editors, the translators; the same persons in both roles give *Edited and translated by*. A fixed order keeps the Org form's three properties lossless.
3. **The affiliations printed are the authors'**, as today; an editor's or translator's are kept and not printed.
4. **One switch, *Editors and translators***, for the two lines in Look, rather than one per role.
5. **The lines are in the language of the document**, English where a language has no words yet; the eight languages that are complete get theirs in this build.
6. **Anonymous hides every contributor.**
7. **The line before is measured**, skipping speakers and directions and empty lines, and an em is added, as editions set the second half a little apart from where the first ended. A line before that wrapped gives too wide a measure; nothing is done about it, the writer sees it.
8. **The step is a length of the layout**, in cm as the other lengths of Look, 0.6 cm where nothing is said; and steps reach the page for every kind, since the text view shows them and the documents follow the preview.
9. ***From here* means from here**: the paragraphs before the cursor keep their numbering, and a change in the middle of a stretch parts it there, the second part going on from the number it had, which the window fills in. The owner named the window so; today's whole-run behaviour remains for the bracket's menu, which begins at the run's first paragraph.
10. **A plain number given by hand follows *every*; a label with letters or signs is always shown.** *Always* and *Never* on the line override both.
11. ***Counted*** is said in the positive, on by default, where the old window said *Not counted*.
12. **The side of the annotations is set in Look for the document**, not stretch by stretch as the numbers' side can be: the numbers' side is a matter of the passage, the annotations' of the page. On the numbers' side they stand outside the numbers.
13. **No key for the setting-in** in this build; the menu item serves, and the owner may ask for a key once they have used it.
14. **The hints of Document properties' Details go** with the others, though the owner named only menus and dialogs: the window is one.
15. **The bracket of text follows the stretch**, and the bracket of any other kind stands outside the numbers along the whole run.

## Why not otherwise

- *A translators field beside the authors*: the owner wants roles now; a list with roles is no more work and takes editors with it.
- *A length for the second half of a shared line*: wrong as soon as the font or the words change; measuring is exact in three of the five kinds of document, and Word and Writer get what their typesetters want.
- *The shared line as one line with two speakers*: it is two speeches, which the speaker of the second begins; what is shared is the count, and which part carries it is the writer's to say.
- *The setting-in making a line uncounted*: traditions differ; the line's window has *Counted*.
- *Tab for the setting-in*: Tab means steps, and steps have their uses on the same line.
- *The whole stretch from the middle*: the window is named *from here*.

## Addendum, 2026-10-24: as built

Built on 2026-10-24 from the plan by two agents in worktrees, one doing stages 1 and 3 (the bracket, the contributors), the other stages 2 and 4 (the numbering, where a line begins), merged into main. What the reproductions showed, and where the build departs from the plan:

- **The bracket**, measured before the fix: six paragraphs of prose with the third and fourth numbered had six segments at two offsets, 10 and 57.8 pixels out from the text; a verse with a speaker among its lines and a line added after it was straight; a verse whose last two paragraphs alone were numbered jumped as the prose did. After the fix the prose has its bracket beside the two numbered paragraphs only, and both verses one offset. The bracket stands outside the numbers where any numbered paragraph of the run stands at the left, as the record says, not by the first one's side, as the plan said; its segments are measured by the size of the text, so that a speaker in a run of a kind set larger stays in line; the menu of a bracket of text is of its stretch.
- **The case of 11b** was reproduced before any change, five lines numbered from 10, every one: given in the margin, through the window, and with the line not counted, the line after *11b* was 12, where one writes and on the page. A stretch of *only the numbers given* counts nothing, by design. With a number shown every fifth line, the lines after *11b* are counted 12, 13 and 14 and not shown, which is the likeliest reading of what the owner saw.
- **The annotations** with the numbers at the right stood at the left, in both views, before any change: across from the numbers, as built.
- **Measuring the line before** was tried by hand first, in Typst (with a speaker's name in small capitals at its head, and in a paragraph set in a step) and in LaTeX (three halves in a row). The measure includes what set that line in, its steps and its own earlier half, so that a line shared by three speakers comes out right; the plan measured the words only. `gk-after` needs no empty box before it.
- **The counters**: the core's gives the writers only what is printed; the interface's gives every number with whether it is printed, so that a number given by hand that the page does not show stands dark in parentheses where one writes.
- **Line numbering from here** writes the number to count from only where the line would not have it by the count already, so that applying the window unchanged adds no marks; its rows *Number from*, *Show a number every* and *Count the lines of* show only for a counted stretch, the side for a counted or a given one. **Numbering of this line** asks only the annotation in a paragraph of a kind the stretch does not count, as for a speaker.
- **An annotation on the numbers' side** stands three em past the number's place in Typst (four in LaTeX), since the plan's four em and more ran off a margin of 2.5 cm; where one writes, six em out at the left, outside the bracket.
- **LaTeX** sets a line of verse in by its steps after the number in the margin, so that the numbers of lines set in stand in the column of the others; a line of verse without a number no longer runs `\noindent` into its first word. Markdown has no steps for paragraphs, and sets in a line that begins where the line before ended with spaces that do not break.
- **The contributors**: the core's `Author` is `Contributor`, with a role that is author where none is said, and a document read with `authors` takes them as contributors. Word's and Writer's own properties have no field for contributors, so the editors and translators are given to Pandoc as `editor` and `translator`, which it writes as custom properties of Word and user-defined properties of Writer; the e-book's package names them with the roles of MARC (`edt`, `trl`); the PDF's properties, the web page's head and Typst's document name the authors only.
- **Words**: `editor-numbers-page`, `editor-numbers-page-hint` and `editor-line-this` are gone, nothing using them; *In a list or a verse: one step in* and *out* for Tab; the two remarks of anonymity say *contributors* under their old names.
- **Where the builds met**: the settings by which paragraphs number together are one list (`STRETCH_ATTRS` in `editor/shape.ts`), read by the bracket, the numbering and Enter alike; the bracket asks the layout only on which side the numbers stand.
