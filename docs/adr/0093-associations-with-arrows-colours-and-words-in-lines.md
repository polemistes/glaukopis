# 0093 — Associations with arrows, colours and words in lines

Date: 2026-11-13. Status: built on 2026-11-13, its choices not yet confirmed by the owner; from the owner's words of the same day ("And it would be nice if the associations could have arrows and colours. And the label of the arrows should be text fileds that can have more than one line."), built from Part C of `docs/plans/export-and-print-from-a-side-a-chat-and-associations-with-arrows.md`. Amends 0041 (the form of `LINKS` in Org) and 0068 (the picture of the diagram, and the arrows of Freeplane).

## Context

- **An association was one dashed gold curve** between two elements in the diagram, and a bracket in the left margin of the text, with a few words on it. It had no direction and no colour: `id`, `from`, `to` and `label` in the project's document. Which end was `from` said only which way it had been drawn, and nothing showed it.
- **Its words were one line.** They were written in a field of one line, in the diagram and in the margin, and every whitespace was made one space when they were kept.
- **Each view had its own menu, or none.** The margin's bracket opened a menu of the two ends to go to, *Add a label…* or *Change the label…*, and *Remove the association*; in the diagram an association was selected by a click, named by a double click and removed by Delete, and had no menu.
- **The ten colours of kinds** (`KIND_COLOURS`) are told apart at a glance in the light and the dark, each with a name the document keeps and a word for it in the interface.

## Decision

1. **An association may have an arrow**: at the end it goes to, at the end it comes from, at both, or none. It is kept as `arrow` on the link, `to`, `from` or `both`, absent for none. A new association has none, as every association made before has. The program gives the direction no meaning; it is the writer's.
2. **An association may have a colour**, one of the colours of kinds, kept by its name as `colour` on the link, absent for the gold it has always had. The curve, its arrowheads, its words and its bracket in the margin take it.
3. **Its words may be in several lines.** When they are kept, spaces and tabs within a line become one space, each line is trimmed, the empty lines at the beginning and the end go, and more than one empty line in a row become one. They are written in a field of several lines that grows with them, in the diagram and in the margin: Enter says they are done, Shift+Enter makes a new line, Escape leaves them as they were, and leaving the field keeps what was written. In the diagram they stand in their lines, centred on the middle of the curve, no wider than 240 pixels, a longer line broken.
4. **One menu of an association**, the same in the diagram and in the margin: in the margin first the two ends to go to; then *Add a label…* or *Change the label…*; **Arrow** with *None*, *To “the element it goes to”*, *To “the element it comes from”* and *Both ways*; **Colour** with *None* and the ten colours, each with its dot; and *Remove the association*. The arrow and the colour that hold are checked. In the diagram it opens on a right click on the curve or its words, which also selects the association, and by the menu key while an association is selected; a click still selects, a double click still writes the words, Delete still removes.
5. **In the diagram an arrowhead is a small filled triangle**, ten pixels long and eight wide, with its tip where the curve meets the box, turned as the curve arrives there. It is a path of its own, not an SVG marker, so that the picture of the diagram reads it as it reads the curve. An association whose end is folded away is drawn faintly to what it is folded under, as before, with its arrowhead.
6. **In the margin of the text** the bracket takes the colour, and an end the arrow points at has a small arrowhead towards the text in place of its dot. Its tooltip writes the ends with the sign of the arrow between them, *A → B*, *A ← B*, *A ↔ B*, and *A – B* where there is none (it said ↔ for every association, which would now mislead), then the words, their lines joined by spaces.
7. **Everything that carries an association carries all of it**: a copy within the project and to another; the picture of the diagram, as SVG and as PNG; the Org copy; the maps of Freeplane and FreeMind; and search, which finds the words whichever line they stand in and writes the ends with the sign of the arrow.
8. **One association between two elements**, as before: drawing one where there is one, either way, gives back the one that is there. *Both ways* is the arrow of a relation that goes both ways.

## Choices made in building this, which the owner has not been asked about

1. **No arrow and gold for a new association**; the arrow and the colour are given in its menu.
2. **The ten colours of kinds rather than any colour**: told apart in every theme, named in the interface, kept by name. A colour that is not one of them, in the document or in a file read, is none.
3. **Enter is done, Shift+Enter a new line**, as everywhere in the application.
4. **The sign *–* between the ends where there is no arrow**, in the tooltip and in search.
5. **The form in Org.** A line break in the words is written `\n` within the link's description, after `\`, `[` and `]` are escaped as before: `[[id:…][son of Peleus\nslayer of Hector]]`. A backslash before any other sign is read as before (the sign alone). The arrow and the colour follow the link in braces, the arrow word and the colour's name, either or both: `[[id:…][answers]]{to teal}`, `[[id:…]]{both}`; nothing where there is neither. A word in the braces that is not an arrow or a colour of kinds is passed over; a file written before reads as it did, and a reader of before, which looks for the links alone, passes over the braces. The comment at the head of `org/properties.ts` says so.
6. **Freeplane.** A connector's heads are written as Freeplane writes them, `STARTARROW` at the node it goes from and `ENDARROW` at the one it goes to, each `NONE` or `DEFAULT` in capitals (the file says it is Freeplane's; FreeMind wrote `None` and `Default`, which are read too); its colour as `COLOR="#rrggbb"`; and a line break in its words as `&#10;` in `MIDDLE_LABEL`, which XML keeps. Where a file read says nothing of the heads, the connector is read as Freeplane and FreeMind draw one that says nothing, as is understood here: a head at its end and none at its start. A colour read is brought to the nearest of the ten when the map becomes a project, as the colour of a kind is, and a connector without one comes in gold. Where a file gives every connector a colour, every association comes in coloured, black as green, the nearest of the ten by measure; whether Freeplane writes a colour for every connector was not seen here.
7. **XMind is left as it was.** The arrows and the colour of a relationship are in its style, whose properties and their values were not known here for certain in both of XMind's forms; nothing is guessed. An association comes in from XMind and goes out to it with its ends and its words only, as before.
8. **The menu keeps the margin's words** *Add a label…*, *Change the label…*, *Remove the association* and *Go to “…”*, under their names in `text.ftl`, so that the languages they are translated into keep them; the words new to it, of the arrow and the colour, are in `project.ftl`, with the words the field had there. *None* among the colours has a dot of the gold the association then has.
9. **Each change in the menu, and the words written, are one step of undo.** Words left as they were make none.
10. **The field of several lines is a widget of its own**, `ui/LinesField.svelte`, which says once that it is done or left; what is written is cleaned by the model.
11. **In search, the lines of the words are read as one line**, so that words that run from one line to the next are found, and what is shown around them is one line.
