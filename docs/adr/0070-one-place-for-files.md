# 0070 — One place for files

Date: 2026-10-27. Status: accepted by the owner on 2026-10-27 ("Yes, make the plan"), from their question whether the files of the library and the store of images should not be one pane, an assessment that agreed, and their resolutions (the caption stays with the document, not the image; no notes on files at all, since notes belong to works; the image notes written so far are discarded; a preview of every kind of file that can be shown; a list and thumbnails of several sizes; a folder listed with or without what lies under it); to be built from `docs/plans/one-place-for-files.md`. Replaces 0010 where it keeps a store of pictures of its own, with captions and notes; amends 0043 (the files of the library: one store, not two; a note on a file is not *later*, it is never), 0046, 0069 (a file in a view: an image is a file of the library), 0036 (the project file carries the images of its figures from the one store), and the record of sharing where it sends the pictures of a project (they are sent as before, from the one store).

## Context

The owner, who has used the two stores, finds the separate place of files and images unintuitive, and asks why the way it is now is better. The answer given in 0043 was about storage: a picture is material of a document, with a caption, sent to those who share the project, travelling in the project file; a file of the library is a source, read, belonging to references, private to this computer. That reasoning decided what the writer sees, and it should not have. Where a file came in from is an accident, not a fact worth organising by: the same image dropped on a text lands among the images, taken in through the files view lands among the files, and the two exist side by side with nothing between them. The e2e script of the viewer shows it: the owl went into the library as a file, and the Images tree stayed empty.

What was found:

- **Two stores, one way of naming.** The library keeps a file as `library/attachments/<first two of the hash>/<hash>/<readable name>` and records it in `library/files.json` (`crates/core/src/library/attachments.rs`, `files.rs`: hash, stored path, name, folder, origin, added). The store of pictures keeps a picture as `pictures/files/<hash>.<png|jpg|svg>` and records it in `pictures/pictures.json` (`crates/core/src/pictures.rs`, `Picture`: hash, extension, name, size, width, height, added, caption, alt, note), with a lazily made thumbnail 480 wide in `pictures/small/`. Both hashes are the SHA-256 of the bytes stored. A picture taken in is prepared first, GIF, WebP, TIFF and BMP made PNG and a JPEG turned upright by its orientation tag, so that the stored bytes, and the hash, may differ from the file's; only PNG, JPEG and SVG are kept. *Take into the pictures* and *Attach to a reference…* copy between the stores (`file_to_picture`, `picture_to_file` in `src-tauri/src/commands/files.rs`), and nothing links the copies after.
- **How figures find their picture.** A figure in a text names its picture by hash and extension (`attrs.file`; `Document::figure_files` in the core); the interface reads it through `pictures.of(hash, extension, small)` (`src/lib/figures/pictures.svelte.ts`) and `picture_read`. The caption of a picture is only a starting point: when a figure is made its words are copied into the document (`src/lib/editor/commands.ts`, `src/lib/figures/views.svelte.ts`), and each figure's caption is the document's own from then on. The alternative text is read from the picture where documents are made. A project says in what is kept about it which pictures it uses (`ProjectInfo.pictures`), so that the store can say where a picture is used without opening the projects.
- **Notes.** A picture has a note of its own in the store, and a project can write a note about it under the key `picture:<hash>` among its notes (`src/lib/pictures/store.svelte.ts`, `noteKey`). A reference has notes in the library and per project too. Nobody has written an image note that must be kept.
- **Sharing and the project file.** A shared project sends the pictures of its figures and takes in what arrives (`sync_pictures` in `crates/core/src/sharing.rs`, over `Pictures::path`, `read`, `keep`); the server keeps files by hash and knows nothing of pictures (`crates/server/src/files.rs`). The project file `.glaukopis` and the readable copy carry the pictures of the figures by hash (`crates/core/src/projectfile.rs`, `readable.rs`). Documents brought in with pictures put them in the store (`crates/core/src/import/document.rs`), and documents made for output read them from it (`crates/core/src/export/mod.rs`, `place_files`).
- **The views.** The library's files view (`src/lib/files/FilesView.svelte`, Ctrl+4) has the tree of folders at the left, the files of the folder in view as rows at the right, filters by kind and *unassigned only*, a search, and a pane of one file (`FilePane.svelte`) with its facts and the actions of its kind. The images view (`src/lib/pictures/PicturesView.svelte`, Ctrl+3) has a grid of thumbnails, a search, and a pane of one image (`PicturePane.svelte`) with its name, caption, alternative text, notes, and the projects that use it. In a project, the picture panel of the document pane (`src/lib/project/PicturePanel.svelte`, Insert › Image, Ctrl+Shift+P) lists the images of this map, of the project or of the whole store, to drag into a text. The viewer of 0069 shows an image from either store (`src/lib/viewer/viewers.svelte.ts` tries the library, then the store), and the chevron over a side lists the two as two trees (`src/lib/viewer/LibraryTrees.svelte`).
- **The drawing of pages.** The core draws a page of a PDF at a width in colour in a child process with bounds, and keeps what it drew in a cache keyed by the file's path, size and time of change, bounded in size (`crates/core/src/ocr/pages_cache.rs`); nothing small is kept of a PDF for good. The thumbnails of pictures are kept in `pictures/small/`.
- **Migrations.** There is no version of the data directory and no migration framework; the one-time move that exists is `Pictures::adopt`, run at the start by `AppState::open` (`src-tauri/src/state.rs`), which took the pictures projects once kept by themselves into the store, and is harmless when there is nothing to move. Unreadable side files are set aside, never deleted (`crates/core/src/sidefile.rs`).

## Decision

### One store

- **The library's store of files holds every file brought in**, images among them: under `library/attachments/<2>/<hash>/<name>` as today, recorded in `library/files.json`. The store of pictures goes. A file's hash is the SHA-256 of the bytes stored, as both stores had it; an image that was a picture keeps its hash, so that every figure in every text finds its image as before.
- **The record of an image** grows by what a figure needs of it: its width and height in pixels, and its alternative text, what it shows in words for those who do not see it. Nothing else: no caption, no note.
- **A file has no note.** Notes belong to works. What there is to say of a file is said in the notes of the reference it belongs to; a file that deserves a note is given a reference first. The notes written on images so far, in the store and under `picture:<hash>` in projects, are discarded at the migration, as the owner has said.
- **The caption is the document's.** A figure's caption is written where the figure stands and nowhere else; a new figure begins with an empty caption. The field on the image goes.
- **Any image of the library can become a figure.** A PNG, a JPEG or an SVG is used as it is. An image of another kind, or a JPEG that lies on its side by its orientation tag, is prepared as the store of pictures prepared every picture, and the prepared bytes become a file of their own in the library, in the same folder, named after the original, its origin saying which file it was made from; the figure names that file. A source is never changed in place. This is the old *Take into the pictures*, done when it is needed and within one store.
- **Where a file is used** is said of it in one list: the references it belongs to, and the projects in which it is a figure, read from what is kept about each project as today. Removing a file says both before it goes.
- **Sharing, the project file, the readable copy and the output are as they were**, reading from the one store: a shared project sends the images of its figures and takes in what arrives; the project file carries them; documents made for output place them.

### One view

- **One Files view, in the library's place** (Ctrl+4), shows every file of the library; the Images view and its place in the rail go, and Ctrl+3 is freed. The view has the tree of folders at the left as today, and at the right the files of the folder in view, **as a list or as a grid of thumbnails in three sizes**, chosen at the head and remembered. The list has the columns a file manager has: name, kind, date added, where it is used, and the folder when the listing reaches into subfolders. The grid shows the thumbnail with the name under it and a small mark where the file is used.
- **Include subfolders**, a switch at the head, off by default, so that a folder looks like a folder. On, the list shows the folder column and the grid the folder under the name.
- **Sorted** by name, by kind or by date added, in both modes; the filters by kind and *unassigned only* and the search stay.
- **A preview in the pane.** The pane of a file shows, above its facts, the file itself small: an image as it is, a PDF by its first page, a document of another kind by its icon. A press on the row opens the pane; Enter, a double-click or *Show* shows the file in a side, as 0069 has it. The small picture of a PDF's first page is drawn once by the core and kept for good beside the file, as the thumbnails of pictures were kept, so that a grid of hundreds does not draw them again; the images' thumbnails are kept the same way.
- **The pane of an image** shows, besides what every file's pane shows, its size in pixels, its alternative text, to be written there, and the projects that use it as a figure, each a link.
- **In a project, the picture panel stays** (Insert › Image, Ctrl+Shift+P): it is about choosing a figure while writing, like the references tab, and lists the images of this map, of the project or of the whole library, from the one store. Adding an image there takes it into the library. Dragging one into a text makes a figure as today.
- **The viewer** of 0069 shows an image as a file of the library, and nothing else; the chevron over a side lists one tree, *Files*, with the images in their folders.

### The migration

- **Once, at the start**, where `pictures/pictures.json` exists: each picture's file is moved into the library's store under its hash, its name kept, and recorded in `files.json` with its date, width, height and alternative text; one already in the library by the same hash is the same file, and only its image facts are added. The moved images stand in one folder, *Images*, made by the migration at the top where there are any, so that a store of many does not land loose at the top; the writer moves them as they like. The captions and notes are not carried. The old directory is set aside as `pictures.old-<date>`, never deleted by the application, and the thumbnails in it are not kept. Said in the log, with the count.
- **A project's notes under `picture:<hash>`** are ignored where they exist and no longer written.

## Choices made in writing this, which the owner has not been asked about

1. The migrated images go into one folder, *Images*, rather than loose at the top. The folder is ordinary once made.
2. The old `pictures/` directory is set aside, not deleted, as every side file is; a later release may remove it.
3. An image of another kind than PNG, JPEG or SVG becomes a figure through a prepared copy that is a file of its own, beside the original; the original is never changed.
4. The project file and the readable copy keep their layout (`pictures/<hash>.<ext>` within them): what a file of the application holds is a format other versions read, and only the store it is taken from changes.
5. The thumbnails are kept beside the files, `library/small/<hash>.png`, 480 wide, made when first asked for, for images and for the first page of PDFs; a thumbnail that cannot be made is the icon, said once in the log.
6. Three sizes of thumbnail in the grid, served by the one 480-wide picture; the list's rows show the small icon of the kind and no thumbnail.
7. Ctrl+3 is left unbound.
8. The picture panel of a project keeps its three scopes, with *the whole store* named *the library*.
9. The sync of a shared project still sends only the images of the project's figures, never the other files of the library.

## Why not otherwise

- **Not three fixed groups, PDF, image and other.** That would rebuild one level down the split the owner dislikes; a filter row and a kind column give the same without the wall.
- **Not notes on files without a reference.** A note written on a file that later gets a reference would have to move or sit beside the reference's note; one rule is better: notes belong to works.
- **Not a default caption on the image.** It said one thing and did another; the figure's caption lives where the figure is.
- **Not the pictures' folder structure on disk.** 0043's reasons hold: the store is addressed by hash, the readable layout is had from the export.
- **Not the images view kept as a filter of the files view.** The grid of thumbnails is a mode of the one view, open to every kind of file, and the filter by kind is there for those who want images only.

## Consequences

- `crates/core/src/pictures.rs` goes; its readers (sharing, the project file, the readable copy, the import of documents, the output, the OCR of a picture, the Tauri state) read the library's store instead. `library/files.json` gains width, height and alt. The `picture_*` commands become commands of the files (`src-tauri/src/commands/pictures.rs`, `files.rs`); the contract follows.
- `src/lib/figures/pictures.svelte.ts` becomes a reading of the files store for figures (thumbnails, sizes, the projects' use); `src/lib/pictures/` goes; `src/lib/files/` gains the modes, the preview, the image's pane; `PicturePanel.svelte`, the viewer, the trees under the chevron, the Split view chooser and the shell's rail, keys and routes follow.
- The guide's section *Images* is folded into *The files of the library*; the words of `pictures.ftl` go or move; the e2e script of the pictures is folded into that of the files.
- Nothing of the model of a document changes: a figure names its file by hash and extension as before.
