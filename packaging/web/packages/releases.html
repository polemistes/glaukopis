Glaukopis, what is new in each version

Be aware: The text below is AI generated, as is the code of Glaukopis. AI code can be good. AI text cannot. The first non-beta release of Glaukopis will not have AI generated texts, except for standard short text for features in the user interface.

Every version so far is put out for testing. The packages of the latest are on the page of packages; the earlier ones are kept on GitHub, under Releases, with their source.

0.2.5 24 October 2026

  • The window made over. The list at the left is one tree of all your folders and projects, with a start screen where no project is open, and the window opens as you left it. Each side of the window holds a project of its own, with its path at the top and a bar of its own; Split view shows two. The pages of a side stand in the typesetting pane, with Look beside them, and can be zoomed; a small label on a line of the page says what it is and opens Look at it.
  • Fewer words. The menus, Look and the settings have lost their help texts: what each thing is called says what it does. An element can be a clone of another, standing in a second place under a name of its own; Time stamp… says when an element is; and the defaults of new documents can be set back to none.
  • Kinds of paragraph by keys. Ctrl+Alt with a digit gives a paragraph the kind its document type gives the digit, Ctrl+Alt+0 being plain text; Look changes them.
  • Editors and translators. A document's people are its contributors, each an author, an editor or a translator, and the title page says Edited by and Translated by in the language of the document.
  • Numbering lines, made plain. Two windows, Line numbering from here and Numbering of this line, without help texts. A number given by hand follows the count of every fifth line as the others do, unless it has letters in it, as 11b; a stretch can count the lines of one kind only; and the annotations of lines can stand in either margin. A line can begin where the line before ended, as the second half of a line of verse shared by two speakers, and how far Tab sets a line or a paragraph in is a length of the layout, kept on the page in every kind of document.
  • Japanese. The interface speaks Japanese, and text in Japanese can be read from scans, written across the page or down it: import the two languages under Settings › Language › Import OCR language files. Nynorsk, Swedish, Danish, German, French and Italian are whole again.
  • The collaboration server for every system. Beside the Arch package, the server comes as a package for Debian and Ubuntu, one for Fedora, and a zip each for Windows and macOS, to be run by hand.
  • Mended. A number given by hand on a later line of a paragraph stands beside its line in the preview and the PDF; a PDF set by LaTeX in Norwegian is made where TeX Live's package of the language is missing; small capitals are made where the font has none; and the line that brackets paragraphs of one kind no longer runs over the numbers of the lines.

0.2.4 21 October 2026

  • Fraktur, Danish and Norwegian, taught on Norwegian print. The language for books in Gothic type printed in Norway and Denmark is trained anew, on some fifteen thousand lines of Norwegian books, periodicals and newspapers of 1811 to 1920 from the National Library, read by eye. On pages of books it never saw it now gets about one letter in three hundred wrong, where the first version got one in forty and the Fraktur model made for German one in twenty-five, and nearly every æ, ø and å right. It is offered under Settings › Language › Import OCR language files, as before.

0.2.3 20 October 2026

  • Reading PDFs and scans, made over. The window opens as a short summary: what the file is, whether its pages take the text they have or are read anew (or each by the quality of its text), and how Tesseract reads them. Look at the pages and try them shows every page, the text a page has beside what Tesseract reads of it, and how sure Tesseract was (low, good or high, in percent), with the words it doubted underlined. Settings can differ from page to page, each trial is kept to compare, and whether a PDF becomes a tree by its headings or by its pages is chosen after it is read.
  • Books in Gothic type. Two new languages for reading scans: Tesseract's Fraktur model, and Fraktur, Danish and Norwegian, taught æ, ø and å for books printed in Norway and Denmark before about 1900. The long s (ſ) is read and kept as s.
  • What you are writing is chosen from one button over the preview, and the Look panel follows the page from top to bottom. About this document holds a document's details and its language.
  • The title page, the abstract and the contents stand where the template puts them, in every format: PDF, Word, Writer, LaTeX, the web page and the e-book.
  • Every line of the preview says what it is. Pressing a line shows a chip naming it, a quotation, a heading, the running head, a page number, and the chip opens Look at it.
  • Headers and footers hold what you put in them, left, centre and right, on the first page and the rest; headings are numbered level by level; and an element with a look of its own keeps it wherever it is printed.
  • Every window can be moved by dragging its title; a double-click on the title puts it back in the middle.
  • The interface is complete in eight languages again: Danish, German, English, French, Italian, Norwegian Bokmål and Nynorsk, and Swedish, with every word of what is new.
  • Faster and steadier. Long texts take typing without delay, large BibTeX files and libraries with many files open at once, and a review of the code fixed some two hundred faults.

0.2.2 18 October 2026

  • PDFs can no longer take the computer down. The text of a PDF is read in a process of its own, which is stopped when it takes too long or too much memory, so that a file the reader loses itself in, as some with an old reading of their text do, costs nothing but its own text. Pages are read by Tesseract only as many at once as the memory free will hold.
  • Reading a PDF anew no longer leaves its old text under the new on pages with pictures the PDF library cannot read, such as those Acrobat's Paper Capture writes.
  • New projects open in the diagram. The settings say whether a new project opens in its diagram or on its text, under New documents. A project opened again opens where it was left.
  • An empty text takes no room. The name of the next element follows closely; a click under a name, or Enter in it, opens its text for writing.
  • The interface is offered in the languages that are complete: Danish, German, English, French, Italian, Norwegian Bokmål and Nynorsk, and Swedish. A system in another language has English until its translation is finished.

0.2.1 17 October 2026

  • The map of the projects, worked as a map. Above every project stands the map of all of them. Projects are drawn as the top elements of their own maps and open as elements do, showing their trees one level at a time; a project is opened at any of its elements from there. Folders and projects are selected, made and named in place, renamed, moved by the keys or by dragging, folded, deleted and put where you like, and the elements of a project's map are moved there as in the project's own map. Every change can be undone from its message or with Ctrl+Z.
  • Pasting on Linux no longer ends the application when the clipboard is empty or the program that holds it does not answer.
  • Menus on Linux no longer flicker on desktops where WebKitGTK 2.54's new compositor showed stale frames: the previous compositor is used.
  • Making a PDF searchable on Windows no longer fails with a missing page file; and where Tesseract cannot make what it was asked for, the message says what Tesseract said.
  • A project is made in a folder, and a folder deleted is brought back whole by undo.

0.2.0 15 October 2026

  • The files of the library. PDFs, pictures and documents kept in folders of their own, with or without a reference, and gone through to find the reference each belongs to. Removing, moving and giving many files is done in one step, and nothing gives one reference to many files at once.
  • Text from PDFs. The text a PDF has is taken, or translated where its fonts keep their letters under other names, as many editions of Greek do, or read anew from pictures of the pages.
  • The structure of a document. A PDF is imported as a map by its title and headings, from the file's own outline, numbered headings or sizes, with lines of verse and numbers in the margin kept, paragraphs joined across pages, and a mark where each page began.
  • The settings ordered anew, with a font of your own for the editor, your details, and credits and licences behind a button.
  • Nothing personal is sent. Glaukopis sends no name or address anywhere by itself.

0.1.0 6 October 2026

The first version put out for testing: references in a library of their own, ideas in maps that are the documents they become, writing, and the manuscript made in the form a publisher asks for. The installers for Windows and macOS brought the dictionaries of spelling and Tesseract's languages with them; from 0.2.0 on they are imported in Glaukopis itself.
