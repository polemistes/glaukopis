Glaukopis, what is new in each version

Be aware: The text below is AI generated, as is the code of Glaukopis. AI code can be good. AI text cannot. The first non-beta release of Glaukopis will not have AI generated texts, except for standard short text for features in the user interface.

Every version so far is put out for testing. The packages of the latest are on the page of packages; the earlier ones are kept on GitHub, under Releases, with their source.

0.2.11 13 November 2026

  • A document is printed and exported from the bar of its side, without the typesetting pane.
  • A shared project has a chat.
  • An association can have an arrow and a colour.
  • The words on an association can be in several lines.

0.2.10 12 November 2026

  • Elements are moved by dragging them in the outline.
  • The wheel zooms the diagram in finer steps.
  • References are copied in the default citation style.
  • A citation is copied in the citation style of its document.

0.2.9 10 November 2026

  • More than one window.
  • Favourite projects.
  • Make any element the document.
  • Choose where everything is kept.
  • The diagram spreads from its book.
  • Notes show their number and text where they stand.
  • Numbered lists from LibreOffice come in as one list.
  • Open projects can be renamed and deleted.
  • Heading numbers shown or hidden in the text view and the diagram separately.
  • A folded heading is marked by … and unfolded from it.
  • Copied text no longer ends in a stray backslash.
  • Typesetting a big book gives its memory back when the pane closes.

0.2.8 31 October 2026

  • A PDF or an image beside your text, in tabs.
  • The files of the library in one place.
  • A simple image editor.
  • Printing.
  • Chapters counted through the parts.
  • The diagram packed closer, saved as SVG or PNG.
  • Mind maps of Freeplane, FreeMind and XMind open as projects.
  • Two lengths of line numbers in Look.
  • Norwegian and Fraktur models for Tesseract improved; their lines transcribed by an AI model (Claude).
  • Intel Macs.

0.2.6 25 October 2026

  • Line numbering put right.
  • The right-click menu of the text is of the line you press.

0.2.5 24 October 2026

  • The window made over, with split view.
  • Fewer words in menus, Look and the settings; clones and time stamps.
  • Kinds of paragraph by Ctrl+Alt and a digit.
  • Editors and translators.
  • Line numbering made plain.
  • Japanese: the interface, and text read from scans.
  • The collaboration server for Debian, Ubuntu, Fedora, Windows and macOS.
  • Small mends in the preview, PDF and LaTeX.

0.2.4 21 October 2026

  • Fraktur, Danish and Norwegian model taught anew on Norwegian print; its lines transcribed by an AI model (Claude).

0.2.3 20 October 2026

  • Reading PDFs and scans made over.
  • Books in Gothic type: two Fraktur models.
  • What you are writing is chosen from one button.
  • Title page, abstract and contents in every format.
  • Every line of the preview says what it is.
  • Headers and footers.
  • Every window can be moved.
  • The interface complete in eight languages.
  • Faster and steadier.

0.2.2 18 October 2026

  • PDFs can no longer take the computer down.
  • Reading a PDF anew leaves no old text behind.
  • New projects can open in the diagram.
  • An empty text takes no room.
  • The interface offered in the languages that are complete.

0.2.1 17 October 2026

  • The map of the projects.
  • Pasting on Linux no longer ends the application.
  • Menus on Linux no longer flicker.
  • Making a PDF searchable works on Windows.
  • A project is made in a folder; undo brings back a deleted folder.

0.2.0 15 October 2026

  • The files of the library.
  • Text from PDFs.
  • The structure of a PDF imported as a map.
  • The settings ordered anew.
  • Nothing personal is sent.

0.1.0 6 October 2026

  • The first version put out for testing: a library of references, ideas in maps that become documents, and manuscripts in the form a publisher asks for.
