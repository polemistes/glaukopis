# The owl

`owl.png` is the mark of Glaukopis: the owl of Athena, drawn by Robert Emil
Berge after a photograph of an Athenian tetradrachm of 454–404 BC (SNG
Copenhagen 39); `owl-icon.png` is the owl's head, drawn by him after the
same, to be seen small: it is the icon of the application and the mark
where the mark is small.

> Classical Numismatic Group, Inc. http://www.cngcoins.com
> <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:SNGCop_039.jpg>

The photograph is under the Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike 3.0
Unported licence, among others, which asks that what is made after it has
the same licence. So the drawing has it: `owl.png`, and everything made of
it, which is `glaukopis.png` here, the icons in `src-tauri/icons/` and the
marks `src/lib/shell/owl.png` and `owl-small.png`, are under

**Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike 3.0 Unported**
(<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/>), the text of which is in
`LICENSE` beside this file.

Who uses the drawing names Robert Emil Berge as the one who drew it, and
Classical Numismatic Group, Inc. (http://www.cngcoins.com) as the source of
what it was drawn after, and says what was changed.

The drawing is not finished, and will be replaced by its author.

## What was done to it

The files made of the drawing are made by `scripts/icons.sh`, which cuts the
drawing to what is drawn in it, sets it in the middle of a square, makes it
smaller, and makes the lines darker where it is very small. For the marks
within the application the white ground is taken away. Nothing is drawn by
the script, and nothing in this directory is made by a machine that draws.
